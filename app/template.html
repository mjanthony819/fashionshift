FashionShift

Classify a garment image with three trained models and route it through a simulated sorting gate, right in your browser - then see exactly how well they work: clean accuracy, calibration, abstention, and behaviour when the images shift.
Benchmark scope: 28×28 grayscale clothing photos from Fashion-MNIST (MIT license, Zalando Research). This is a model-audit bench, not a real-world photo classifier or a shopping recommender.

What this can and cannot tell you

The models know ten classes of 28×28 grayscale catalog clothing photos. A phone photo, a drawing, or a different garment style is out of distribution: expect confident nonsense. That is exactly the behaviour this workbench exists to expose.

Sorting-gate simulator, benchmark data only. The gate routes each garment to an auto-sort bin (its predicted class) when confidence clears the threshold, otherwise to manual review - the same way a confidence-gated conveyor line would act. This is a study in routing logic, not factory-ready QC: on 28×28 benchmark photos, confidence does not prove reliable physical sorting, defect detection, or calibrated safety. Under shifted inputs the models stay confidently wrong (see the stress lab), so a real gate misroutes exactly when it looks sure.
